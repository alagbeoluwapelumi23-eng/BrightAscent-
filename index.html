Portfolio preview

Three writing samples from different industries, showing the range of voice we write in.

Financial ServicesProfile: Chief Financial Officer at a regional bank
Objective: Build credibility with institutional partners by explaining decisions in plain language

The question I ask before every budget meeting

Most budgets do not fail because the numbers are wrong. They fail because the people reading them are unsure what decision they are being asked to make. Before any budget meeting, I write one sentence: what do I need this room to decide?
Writing sample

HealthcareProfile: Director of Clinical Operations at a private hospital group
Objective: Position as an operations leader who protects both patients and teams

Good scheduling is a patient safety issue

A rota is never just a rota. When a nurse is stretched across too many shifts, patients feel it before any report does. The most useful change we made was not a new system. It was asking the people on the ward what the schedule was costing them.
Writing sample

TechnologyProfile: Founder of a B2B software company
Objective: Build trust with customers and investors ahead of a new market entry

What we learned by saying no to a large prospect

The deal looked perfect on paper. It would also have pulled our product in three directions at once. Saying no was uncomfortable, but it taught our team what we are actually building, and for whom.
